# Student walkthrough recording

Recording completed by Diego on October 24, 2026. The reviewed [walkthrough GIF](media/walkthrough.gif) is embedded in the README. The steps below document the recording sequence.

Diego must record the walkthrough himself. This guide is preparation only; it does not record, upload, publish, or submit anything.

## Before recording

1. Open the Flashcard project in Xcode and run it on the iPhone simulator.
2. If you are partway through a round, sort the remaining cards and choose **Reset All Cards** to begin with the complete deck.
3. Keep the whole app visible, including **Diego Lopez · Z23661298** at the bottom.
4. Start your own screen recording using macOS Screenshot (**Shift–Command–5**) or your preferred recorder. Record only the simulator area if possible.

## Required sequence

1. Pause briefly on the main screen so the deck and student footer can be read.
2. Tap the first card to reveal the answer, then tap again to return to its question.
3. Slowly drag a card a short distance left and release it before the threshold. Show the red **MORE PRACTICE** feedback and spring-back without losing the card.
4. Swipe that card fully left. Show the practice count increasing.
5. Slowly drag the next card right, then finish the swipe. Show the green **MEMORIZED** feedback and the memorized count increasing.
6. Swipe through the remaining cards using both directions until **Round complete!** appears.
7. Tap **More Practice**. Show that only the practice cards return and the memorized count stays intact. Sort those cards.
8. Tap **Reset All Cards** and show that the complete saved deck returns with the reviewed counters cleared.
9. Tap **+**. Show that Save is initially disabled. Enter a question and answer, tap **Save**, and show your new card at the front. Tap it to show the answer.

## Optional bonus sequence

1. Close and relaunch the app to demonstrate that your custom card and study progress remain saved.
2. Open **Manage cards** (the stacked-card icon). Swipe your own disposable demonstration card left to reveal Delete, choose Delete, and confirm its removal. Close the library.
